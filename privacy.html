Privacy policy

Harborwake · Effective 22 November 2026

The short version

Harborwake collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your game in progress (the board, each player's goods and cards, the players' names and your settings, such as music volume and map rules) in the app's own storage on your device, so you can continue later. This data never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. The saved game is removed when a game finishes, and everything is removed when you delete the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage data or crash data. The app contains no third-party code that does so.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). This meets the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

This website

These pages use no cookies, trackers or web fonts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date before the change applies.
