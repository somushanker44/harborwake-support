Harborwake

Call the seasons. Settle the isle. Open your harbors.

Harborwake is a hex island strategy game for 2 to 6 players. Play against computer opponents, or pass one device around for local multiplayer. There are no ads, no purchases, no accounts and no internet connection needed.

Questions

How do I play?

Tap How to play on the main menu for the full rules. In short: on your turn you call a season, then build and trade, then end your turn. Camps and harbors are worth 1 point, keeps 2, and two bonuses are worth 2 each. The first player to reach the points target on their own turn wins.

There are no dice. How do I get goods?

Tap Call a season and pick a land. Every building beside a tile of that land collects its good. You collect the full amount shown on the tile; the other players collect 1 less from each tile. The list shows what everyone would get before you choose.

Why are some tiles dimmed with 💤?

Those lands are resting. The two lands called most recently cannot be called again until other seasons have been called.

What does the Storm do?

About once a round the player whose turn it is must move the Storm to a new tile. That tile gives nothing while the Storm is on it, and each rival beside it loses 1 of that tile's good. The badge at the top left of the board shows when the Storm moves next.

Why did I have to throw goods back?

You may keep 10 goods at the end of your own turn, plus 2 more for each harbor you own. Spend or trade the rest first, or choose which to throw back.

How do I build a harbor?

Tap one of your camps that sits on the coast and choose Harbor, or tap Build and then Harbor. A harbor lets you trade 2 goods for 1 at the bank instead of 3 for 1.

How do I trade with other players?

Tap Trade, pick what you give and what you want, and tap Offer. If you only pick what you want and tap Ask, each rival proposes a price. You then choose who to trade with.

How do I play with friends?

On the main menu, raise Humans above 1. Players take turns on the same device, and the game hides each hand until that player takes the device.

Can I change the computer opponents, the map or the length of the game?

Yes. On the main menu, tap an opponent to swap in another character or tap Shuffle, switch the Map rules on or off, and set Points to win from 10 to 14.

How do I turn the music down or off?

Tap Music on the main menu, or the ☰ button during a game and then Music. The music also follows your device's silent switch.

Will I lose my game if I close the app?

No. The game saves on your device after every move. Open the app and tap Continue saved game.

How do I delete my saved game?

A finished game is removed automatically. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device.
